Enquiries

Frequent questions about DitchFees.

Got questions about how surcharging works or how to set up your zero-fee terminal? Here are quick answers to our most common enquiries.

Are quotes and initial terminal consultations really free? +

Yes, 100% free with zero obligation. We audit your current merchant statements and show you exactly how much money you will save by switching to zero-fee surcharging.

Where do you operate and who can sign up? +

We service businesses Australia-wide, from brick-and-mortar retail storefronts and cafes to mobile tradies, service providers, and online merchants.

How does the $0 terminal rental threshold work? +

Terminal rental is $0 per month when your terminal transacts at least $12,000 in monthly volume. If your monthly turnover falls below $12,000 in any month, a low fee of $29 (inc. GST) applies for that month.

Are there any lock-in contracts or exit penalties? +

No lock-in contracts and no hidden exit fees. We believe in keeping your business through great service and zero monthly bills, not restrictive contracts.

How quickly do sales funds settle into my bank account? +

Your funds are settled daily via 100% net settlement directly into your existing Australian bank account, so you never have to deal with monthly transaction bills.
